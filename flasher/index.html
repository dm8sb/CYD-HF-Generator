How to flash

  1. Use Chrome or Edge on a desktop computer (Windows, macOS, Linux). Firefox, Safari and mobile browsers cannot flash.
  2. Connect the board by USB. On Windows, the USB serial chip of the CYD (CH340) needs the CH340 driver from WCH if no COM port appears.
  3. Click Connect and choose the board's serial port (Windows: COMx, "USB-SERIAL CH340"; Linux: /dev/ttyUSB0).
  4. First install: confirm Erase. This clears the settings memory: touch calibration, frequency correction, WiFi and all saved settings. The touch calibration wizard starts after the first boot.
  5. Update: do not erase. The bootloader, partition table and firmware are rewritten; touch calibration, correction, WiFi and settings are kept.

If the connection fails, hold the BOOT button while clicking Connect, close any serial monitor that holds the port, and try another USB cable (some cables carry power only).
